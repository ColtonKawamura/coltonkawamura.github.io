**TL;DR:** Sound waves in granular materials don't just fade smoothly—they switch between two completely different behaviors at a critical frequency. And jamming is the key.

## The Problem

How does sound get damped in fluid-saturated granular materials? Scientists have measured it. Physics couldn't fully explain it until now.

## The Solution: Jammed-Network Scattering (JNS)

We show that at a critical frequency (which depends on pressure), wave behavior flips:

- **Below critical freq**: Coherent waves, viscous-like damping  
- **Above critical freq**: Sound fragments into scattered particle motion

The closer the material is to jamming, the sharper this transition.

## Why It Matters

- Predicting seismic wave behavior in soils
- Designing acoustic filters and dampening materials
- Understanding how granular materials respond to vibrations
