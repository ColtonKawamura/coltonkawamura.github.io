[**Who's still in?**](https://coltonkawamura.github.io/whos-still-in/) is an unofficial site where U.S. Naval Academy graduates can see what share of their commissioning class has self-reported as still serving, out, or unreported, and voluntarily report their own status through a GitHub issue form.

Nothing is estimated or modeled: each class's denominator is its documented commissions from cited public sources, and everyone who hasn't reported is counted as unreported. Only aggregate counts are published. Source: [ColtonKawamura/whos-still-in](https://github.com/ColtonKawamura/whos-still-in).
